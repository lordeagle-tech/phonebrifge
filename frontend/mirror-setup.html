🪞 One-Click Phone Mirror

Mirrors your full Android screen on this laptop with mouse + keyboard control, using the system scrcpy tool. Open this page from a browser on the laptop itself.

1. Dependencies scrcpy: ? adb: ? sndcpy: ? vlc: ?
scrcpy and adb are missing. On Parrot/Debian/Ubuntu:
sudo apt install scrcpy adb
Run that in a laptop terminal, then click Recheck.

3. Pair a phone wirelessly
Pair with a QR code (Android 11+) Idle
  1. On your phone: Settings → System → Developer options → Wireless debugging → Pair device with QR code.
  2. Tap Show QR code below, then point your phone's camera at this screen.
  3. The bridge auto-detects, pairs, and connects — no typing needed.
Or pair manually with a 6-digit code
💡 Tip: open Phone Bridge on your phone and look at the “Pair this phone” card on the Stats page — it shows the phone's IP and a one-tap copy button so you don't have to read it off the phone's Settings screen.
  1. On your phone: Wireless debugging → Pair device with pairing code.
  2. Your phone shows an IP & port and a 6-digit pairing code.
  3. Type them below and tap Pair. (You only do this once per phone.)
  4. Then enter the regular Wireless-debugging IP+port (different from the pairing one) and tap Connect.
Line the phone's “Pair this phone” QR up inside the square.
